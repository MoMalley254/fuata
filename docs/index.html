Money tracker

Follow your money.

Fuata is a small Android app that shows you, at a glance, what you have across cash, mobile money, banks and cards — how much you've spent today, who it went to, and what's left.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server. The version of Fuata distributed on Google Play has no internet permission, so nothing you type can leave the device.

What it does

  • Units. Name your own places for money — Cash, M-PESA, a bank, a card — with an emoji and a colour. Add or archive them at any time.
  • Today at a glance. A ring on the home screen answers "am I okay today?" against your daily limit, in one of four states: safe, close, almost, over.
  • A banner in your notifications. The same number, visible without opening the app. Optional, and separate from the app itself.
  • Catch payments from messages. You teach Fuata what one kind of bank or mobile-money message looks like by labelling the parts that matter. After that, it can turn those messages into pending transactions for you to check. This is optional, off by default, and only ever looks at apps you choose.
  • Summaries. Today, this week, this month — what you spent, what you got, and where it went.
  • Your own backups. Export everything to a single JSON file wherever you choose. Restore when you need to.

What stays on the phone

All of it. Units, transactions, limits, message-reading rules, and settings live in a local database on the device. Fuata does not send analytics, crash reports, or any other telemetry. If you never use the Back up & restore feature, your data never leaves the device.

Read the full policy. Fuata's privacy policy explains in plain language what is stored, what is read, and what is never touched.

What it doesn't do

  • No bank passwords, card numbers, or account numbers are ever asked for.
  • No adverts, no in-app purchases, no upsells.
  • No accounts, no cloud sync, no "sign in with".

Availability

Fuata is built for Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. It will be available on Google Play. While it is in development, the source is on GitHub.
