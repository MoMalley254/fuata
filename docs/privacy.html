Privacy policy

Fuata keeps your money private.

Fuata is a local-first Android money tracker. It has no account, no server, and no internet permission in the version distributed on Google Play. Nothing you type, capture or save in Fuata is sent anywhere by the app.

Effective date: 21 October 2026
Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary for Google Play

Data collected by the developerNone. There is no server to send it to.
Data shared with third partiesNone.
Data stored on your deviceYes — units, transactions, limits, rules and settings, in a local database inside the app's private storage.
Data encryptedYes. Fuata uses SQLCipher for the local database and stores the key in Android Keystore. Scheduled backup files can be passphrase-encrypted.
Users can delete their dataYes, via Settings → Privacy & data → Erase everything. There is nothing to request from us.

1. Who we are

Fuata is an independent Android app built by Kinyua Nexus. Questions about this policy should go to kinyuanexus@gmail.com.

2. What Fuata does not collect

  • No account, email address, or phone number to sign in with.
  • No analytics, crash reports, or telemetry of any kind.
  • No advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • No access to the network from the release build. The version distributed on Google Play does not request the INTERNET permission.

3. What Fuata stores on your phone

Fuata keeps its own data in a local database inside the app's private storage on your phone. This is:

  • Units (cash, mobile money, banks, cards) with balances.
  • Transactions (spent, got, moved), including who they were with and when.
  • Limits and alert settings.
  • Categories.
  • Message-reading rules (templates) you build.
  • Settings: your name, accent colour, theme, banner options.
  • Optionally, a short list of unreadable messages, kept only so you can teach Fuata to read them. These are deleted after 14 days or when you dismiss them, whichever comes first.

This data does not leave the phone unless you use one of the export features described in section 5.

4. Notification access (optional, off by default)

Fuata can optionally read notifications from apps you pick, in order to catch payments. This is one of Android's most sensitive permissions, so here is exactly what happens:

  • You choose which apps Fuata may read. Nothing else is read — notifications from any other app are dropped the moment they arrive, before Fuata sees their text.
  • Fuata reads the notification text only from those apps.
  • If a notification looks like a payment, Fuata keeps it so you can review it. If it doesn't, Fuata drops it immediately and does not store it.
  • One-time codes (OTPs, PINs, verification codes) are always dropped, even when the surrounding message also looks financial.
  • You can switch this off at any time in Fuata's settings, or revoke notification access in Android's own Settings → Notification access.

5. Backups

You can export everything as a single JSON file to a location you choose — for example, Google Drive, a USB stick, or your Downloads folder. Fuata does not send this file anywhere; it writes it to the folder you picked using Android's own system file picker.

Scheduled backups encrypt the file before writing it, using a passphrase you choose. Fuata does not store that passphrase — if you lose it, the file cannot be opened.

6. App lock

If you turn on App lock, Fuata asks Android to unlock the device before showing your money. It uses Android's own biometric and screen-lock mechanisms; Fuata does not store, see, or transmit your fingerprint, face data, or PIN.

7. Children's privacy

Fuata does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is designed for adults managing their own money. It does not knowingly handle any information about children.

8. Your rights

Because Fuata holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing to request, export, or delete from us. Everything is on your phone and under your control:

  • To see what is stored, open Fuata.
  • To take it with you, use Settings → Privacy & data → Back up & restore.
  • To delete it, use Settings → Privacy & data → Erase everything. That wipes every unit, transaction, rule and setting from the phone.

9. Changes to this policy

If Fuata changes how it handles data — for example, if a future version adds an optional sync feature — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date will change. The policy will keep describing the version of Fuata that is currently on Google Play.
